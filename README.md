# JUKEBOX AUDIOPLAYER WEBSITE
This is a website that allows you to add your own audio files and play them and control with fancy controls, The theme of the webpage is desert inspired by "DUSTLINE" of Pixl(hackclub event)
<img width="1032" height="705" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b1b867ae-4ad7-4371-a70f-c6118f37bc3a" />


## How to use?
The website is very clean and easy to use, you have three control buttons at bottom PLAY,FORWARD AND BACKWARD, as It suggests they play/pause, Plays next song, plays previous song respectively, has a Plus icon at top you can click to add your own song or audio files, A good songlist/playlist ready to be clicked and player , a volume slider to control the volume going up

## KNOWN ISSUES:
* The play icon does not change to pause icon\
* the ADD Audio box is really plain looking
* The volume button does not mute
