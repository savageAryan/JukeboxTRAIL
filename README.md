## PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
A jukebox website designed for playing your audio files without downloading anything, It has a ready library of songs to choose from while also allowing to add your own audio files, Runs on your browser and easy to use, Currently advised to be used in chrome on a desktop display

## RequireMents:
* A web-Browser (preferably [Chrome](https://www.google.com/intl/en_in/chrome/))
## How to use?
The website is very clean and easy to use, you have three control buttons at bottom PLAY,FORWARD AND BACKWARD, as It suggests they play/pause, Plays next song, plays previous song respectively, has a Plus icon at top you can click to add your own song or audio files, A good songlist/playlist ready to be clicked and player , a volume slider to control the volume going up

## KNOWN ISSUES:
* The play icon does not change to pause icon\
* the ADD Audio box is really plain looking
* The volume button does not mute
* Some Features only work for chrome
* Designed according to Desktop, Smaller displays may look weird
